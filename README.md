# Signal Generator & Power Electronics Circuit Designs

A collection of power electronics and control circuit designs for generating, conditioning, isolating, and driving electrical signals used in inverter and power-conversion applications.

The project covers a range of circuit topologies, including:

- Square-wave signal generators for producing digital switching and gate-drive signals.
- Triangular-waveform generators for carrier-signal generation and PWM applications.
- Sinusoidal-waveform generators for producing reference signals used in AC and inverter control systems.
- Isolated driver circuits for providing galvanic isolation between control circuitry and high-power switching stages.
- Inverter circuits for converting DC power into controlled AC output.
- Phase-shift signal generation for creating precisely delayed or shifted control signals required by full-bridge, half-bridge, and other switching topologies.
- Gate-drive and switching circuits for controlling power semiconductor devices.
- Additional supporting circuits for signal conditioning, timing, control, and power-electronics experimentation.

The designs are intended to provide practical building blocks for developing and experimenting with inverter systems, PWM controllers, isolated power stages, signal-generation circuits, and other power-electronics applications.

The repository contains circuit schematics and related design resources that can be used as a reference for understanding, prototyping, modifying, and integrating these circuits into larger power-electronics systems.
